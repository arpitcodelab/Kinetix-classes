## 1. Executive Summary & Site Architecture

Kinetix Classes is an offline science and competitive exam coaching institute located in Alpha 2, Greater Noida. The source website (`https://kinetix-classes-for.grexa.site/`) is an automated site generated by **Grexa AI** from Kinetix Classes' **Google Business Profile** (Place ID: `ChIJC5tZtyfrDDkR2WHg2F06RR8`).

While the current site succeeds in establishing basic local presence and capturing verified customer reviews, it suffers from severe limitations:
1. **Generic Site-Builder Template:** Uses an auto-generated Next.js / Grexa layout with repetitive section copies and stock clip art.
2. **Missing Essential LMS & Academic Architecture:** No syllabus downloads, student portal, timetable, fee calculator, or batch schedules.
3. **No Direct Faculty Attribution:** Promotes "IITians & Doctors" as faculty, but provides no names, photos, or biographies.
4. **Resolution Bottleneck:** No desktop-ready hero photography exists (maximum photo width is 512px).

The rebuild will transform this raw content into a high-performance, fast, accessible, and LMS-ready modern web experience with a distinct kinetic visual identity.

3. **Class 9th Foundation Coaching:** Mathematics and Science foundation to bridge the transition to senior secondary science.

## 5. Contradictions, Typos, and Outdated Content (Flagged)

The following items are flagged directly from the source text and must be corrected or verified with the client:

1. **Unrealistic 24/7 Operating Hours:**
   - Source lists `Monday-Sunday: 24 hours` in multiple dialogs and headers.
   - For an offline educational institute with physical premises, 24/7 hours is misleading to parents who need to know real counseling/reception desk timings (e.g., 9:00 AM – 7:30 PM).
2. **Spelling & Grammatical Typos in Source Copy:**
   - Heading on Home and Products: `"NDA Coaching classess"` *(misspelled "classess")*.
   - Copy in NDA card: `"NDA Coaching Greater Noida. NDA Preparation Defence Exam Coaching Defence Exam Coaching"` *(duplicated "Defence Exam Coaching")*.
   - Heading on Services: `"ICSE Physics , Chemistry , Maths , Biology Classes"` *(unnecessary space before comma: `"Physics ,"`)*.
   - Post Title: `"Neet classes start from 1 st july .."` *(awkward space before ordinal: `"1 st"`, and trailing double period `".."`)*.
3. **Outdated Announcements & Stale Dates:**
   - The only announcement post on the site is `"Neet classes start from 1 st july .."` with a July 3 date. There are zero active announcements for upcoming batches, crash courses, or test series.
4. **Third-Party Stock Imagery:**
   - The 8 product card images are low-res web thumbnails (`encrypted-tbn0.gstatic.com`) representing generic clip art and stock photos. These must be replaced with custom branded graphics.
5. **Photo Count Discrepancy:**
   - The button on Home and About claims `"View all photos (18)"`, but only 10 photo assets are present in the DOM.
6. **Unsubstantiated Academic Claims:**
   - Copy claims `"Affordable Fee Structure: Low fee with Scholarship Test + Easy EMI option"` — but provides zero fee numbers, EMI partner details, or scholarship dates.
   - Copy claims `"IITians & Doctors with 8+ years of NEET/JEE teaching experience"` — but names zero faculty members.
