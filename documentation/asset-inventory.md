## 2. Usability Assessment & Replacement Strategy

### 2.1 Genuine Brand Assets (Usable)
- **10 Classroom Photos (`photo_1` to `photo_10`):** These are genuine photos of the institute, its classrooms, whiteboards, students, and exterior signage in Alpha 2, Greater Noida. They provide authentic local trust proof. In Phase 2, they will be converted to modern WebP/AVIF formats with responsive `srcset` and subtle modern framing.
- **Brand Avatar (`logo_avatar`):** Authentic symbol of Kinetix Classes. In Phase 2, we should create a crisp SVG vector mark based on this identity.
- **Reviewer Avatars:** Authentic Google user photos linked to the 10 five-star reviews.

### 2.2 Flagged Third-Party Stock Graphics (Do NOT Reuse As-Is)
- All 8 product card images (`prod_12pcb`, `prod_10board`, `prod_11pcm`, `prod_neet`, `prod_12pcm`, `prod_11pcb`, `prod_iitjee`, `prod_class9`) are third-party stock photos and web thumbnails (`encrypted-tbn0.gstatic.com`). Reusing them will make the new site look like a cheap template.
- **Proposed Solution for Phase 2 / Phase 3:** Replace generic stock thumbnails with custom branded CSS/SVG scientific illustrations (e.g., stylized trajectory vectors for JEE, clean molecular structures for NEET, clean geometric coordinate systems for Class 9–10 Boards) aligned with the "Kinetix" motion brand identity.

### 2.3 Missing High-Resolution Hero Visuals
- The source website possesses **zero** high-resolution hero images (the largest authentic classroom photo is only 512px wide).
- Stretched across a 1440px desktop screen, existing photos become blurry and pixelated.
- **Proposed Solution:** In Phase 3 (Design Direction), propose a bold typographic hero with interactive kinetic physics/particle lines (via CSS/GSAP canvas/SVG) or an authentic layered collage framing the classroom photos at their native high-density resolution, rather than blowing up low-res photos.
